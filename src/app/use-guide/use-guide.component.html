Where can I find Sponsored Tasks?
Look for the “Sponsored Ad” label

Sponsored Tasks are normally displayed on the first page of the Daily Posts section. They may also appear between posts or on other pages across the platform.

What is the difference between Daily Engagement and Sponsored Tasks?
Daily Engagement Free participation

Daily Engagement is available without activating a plan. Users are encouraged to read selected posts, react naturally and leave meaningful comments. This helps the platform maintain a genuine, active audience and produce reliable engagement data.

Sponsored Tasks Plan required

Sponsored Tasks are campaigns provided by participating organizations. Eligible users can earn up to $0.20 for each sponsor task clicked.

All activity must be genuine. Automated clicks, repeated actions and meaningless engagement will not be rewarded.

Is the daily reward guaranteed?

No. The displayed amount is a potential reward range. Eligibility depends on completing the daily requirements and passing activity verification.

Why must I read and comment on posts?

Sponsors need genuine participation rather than empty clicks. Meaningful engagement produces more reliable campaign results.

Do I need a plan for daily engagement?

Daily engagement and plan-based sponsor tasks are separate opportunities. A plan is required only for tasks marked as plan-exclusive.

When will my reward appear?

A completed activity may first appear as pending. It is added to your available balance after verification.
